# Frontier AI hiring signals

This documentation summarizes a reproducible 2026-11-04 snapshot of role-title signals from 29 configured official applicant-tracking-system boards and 6,864 open postings.

| Role family | Matching engineering roles | Companies represented |
|---|---:|---:|
| Search | 22 | 4 |
| Agents | 21 | 3 |
| Hardware / silicon | 19 | 3 |
| Reinforcement learning | 4 | 1 |

## What this measures

- Open postings visible during the snapshot window.
- Conservative role-title matches for four overlapping role families.
- Company representation within each role-family scan.

## What this does not measure

- A posting is not a completed hire.
- The role families overlap and must not be summed as unique roles.
- Counts do not measure team size, hiring velocity, or future headcount plans.
