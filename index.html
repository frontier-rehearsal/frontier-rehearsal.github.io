What this measures

The snapshot counts engineering postings whose titles matched four declared role families. Each posting is counted once within a family. The scans overlap, so the four totals must not be added to infer a unique-role total.

What this does not measure

  • Postings are not completed hires or headcount growth.
  • Conservative title matching misses relevant work hidden under broad titles.
  • Counts do not establish that one field is proportionally “hotter” than another.

Reproduce and explore

The aggregate tables, company-level counts, and full method are published openly. Frontier Rehearsal is independent and is not affiliated with the companies represented in the source data.
